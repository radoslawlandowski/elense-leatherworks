Edge finishing has a direct impact on appearance, comfort, and durability in leather goods.
It is a small detail that often defines whether a piece feels truly premium.

## 1. Painted edge

The edge is sanded, sealed, and coated with dedicated edge paint.
It creates a clean modern line and works especially well in minimal product designs.

## 2. Burnished edge

In this method, the edge is compressed and polished to a smooth satin finish.
It is a classic approach that highlights the natural character of leather.

## 3. Natural sealed edge

This option keeps a more natural look while still protecting the edge against fraying and moisture.
It is useful in products with a more technical or utility-oriented design.

## How to choose the right finish?

The best method depends on how the product will be used and the look you want to achieve.
In our workshop, edge finishing is selected based on leather type, daily usage, and expected visual effect.
