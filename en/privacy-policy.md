**Last updated: July 7, 2026**

## 1. Data Controller

The data controller for personal data collected through elenseleatherworks.com is Radosław Landowski, trading as Elense Leatherworks. Contact for data protection matters: [radoslaw@elenseleatherworks.com](mailto:radoslaw@elenseleatherworks.com).

## 2. What Data We Collect

Depending on how you use the site, we may process:

- **Order information** (name, shipping address, email address, phone number) — handled through our cart and payment provider (Snipcart).
- **Contact details**, if you reach out to us by email or WhatsApp.
- **Analytics data** about site traffic (e.g. Google Analytics, if enabled), collected in aggregated form.
- **Cookies** required for the shopping cart to work, and, if you consent, analytics cookies.

We do not collect special categories of data and do not perform automated profiling that affects your rights.

## 3. Purposes and Legal Basis

| Purpose | Legal basis |
|---|---|
| Order fulfillment and handling complaints | Performance of a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) |
| Responding to contact inquiries | Legitimate interest of the controller (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)) |
| Marketing and managing the brand's social media presence (Pinterest, Instagram, Etsy) | Legitimate interest of the controller (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)) |
| Website traffic analysis | Consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)), where analytics cookies are used |

## 4. Recipients of Data

Data may be shared with trusted service providers who process it on our behalf:

- **Snipcart** — cart and payment processing.
- **Etsy** — if you purchase through our Etsy shop, Etsy's own privacy policy also applies.
- **Pinterest and Meta (Instagram)** — to the extent we publish and manage promotional content on these platforms; we do not share customer personal data with them, we use their APIs solely to publish marketing content.
- **Google Analytics** — if active, for website traffic analysis.

We do not sell personal data to third parties.

## 5. Data Retention

Order-related data is kept for the period required by tax and accounting regulations. Contact data is kept until the matter you contacted us about is resolved, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

## 6. Your Rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to:

- access your data and receive a copy of it,
- rectify (correct) your data,
- erase your data,
- restrict processing,
- data portability,
- object to processing based on legitimate interest,
- lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority (in Poland: UODO).

To exercise these rights, contact us at [radoslaw@elenseleatherworks.com](mailto:radoslaw@elenseleatherworks.com).

## 7. Cookies

The site uses cookies necessary for the shopping cart to function, and, optionally, analytics cookies. You can manage cookies in your browser settings.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always available at this address, with the last-updated date at the top of the page.
