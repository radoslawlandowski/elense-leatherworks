My name is Radosław Landowski. Since 2020 I have been designing and hand-stitching accessories from vegetable-tanned leather, using historical leathercraft techniques.

## How the products are made

I cut and stitch every piece by hand, from start to finish in Poland. Vegetable-tanned leather is a natural material that develops a patina over time, so each piece matures with the way you use it.

## Elense Experience

Elense Experience is a design philosophy built on three pillars: lasting craftsmanship, personalization and everyday comfort. From cutlery pockets and coasters to trays and organizers, every product exists so that ordinary gestures bring more satisfaction.

## Personalization and warranty

Selected products are made with an individual detail, agreed before you order. Every product comes with a 12-month workmanship warranty: if there is a workmanship defect, I repair or replace it.
